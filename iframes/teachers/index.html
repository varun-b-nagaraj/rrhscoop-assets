02 / From our store to your door

Stay in your flow.
We’ll come to you.

CO-OP student associates deliver to classrooms and department workrooms during designated class periods or conference times.

Shop the CO-OP Or contact the store to arrange an order.

03 / A little permission goes a long way

Send them with
a blue pass.

Blue passes let eligible students visit the CO-OP during designated class periods without being marked out of area.

Teachers can authorize store errands and visits during appropriate non-instructional intervals. Complete the interest form to request a blue CO-OP hall pass.

Request a blue pass (opens in a new tab)

Have your name, school email, and room number ready. The form says passes will be distributed in the coming weeks.

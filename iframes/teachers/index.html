03 / A little permission goes a long way

Send them with
a blue pass.

Blue passes let eligible students visit the CO-OP during designated class periods without being marked out of area.

Authorize store errands and visits during appropriate non-instructional intervals, then use the Virtual Pass Dashboard to follow your students’ activity.

Request a blue pass

Complete the interest form with your name, school email, and room number to request a blue CO-OP hall pass. The form includes current distribution information.

Virtual Pass Dashboard

(BETA) View your students’ CO-OP activity, including all check-ins and the activity data logged when they use your pass.
